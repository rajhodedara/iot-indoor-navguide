# User Guide

Welcome to the Indoor Navigation App. This guide is written to help you understand how to use the app to navigate the university building independently and safely.

## 1. Getting Started

### Installation and Login
When you open the app for the first time, you will be prompted to log in. 
- The login screen is completely screen-reader friendly.
- Swipe right to move through the email and password fields, and double-tap to enter your information.
- Once logged in, the app will ask for permission to access your Location, Microphone, and Bluetooth. **You must allow these** for the navigation to work.

## 2. Navigation Experience

### How to Start
When you enter the department building, simply open the app. The app will quietly vibrate to let you know it is running. Once it detects the entrance, it will announce your starting location.

### Audio Guidance
The app will speak directions to you, such as:
- "Walk straight for about 10 meters."
- "Turn left at the upcoming intersection."
- "You have arrived at Room 102."

### Haptic (Vibration) Feedback
You can keep the phone in your pocket and rely on vibrations:
- **One short vibration**: Keep going straight.
- **Two short vibrations**: Turn Left.
- **Three short vibrations**: Turn Right.
- **One long, continuous vibration**: You have arrived at your destination, or you need to stop.

## 3. Voice Commands

You do not need to look at the screen to use the app. Simply **double-tap anywhere on the screen** to activate the microphone. Wait for the short beep, then speak.

**Examples of what you can say:**
- *"Take me to Room 105."*
- *"Take me to the nearest restroom."*
- *"Where am I?"*
- *"Cancel navigation."*
- *"Read my schedule."*

## 4. Emergency SOS

Your safety is the priority. If you are lost, feel unsafe, or need immediate assistance, you can trigger the SOS mode.

**How to trigger:**
- **Press and hold** anywhere on the screen for 3 seconds.
- OR double-tap and say, *"Help me"* or *"Emergency."*

**What happens:**
The app will immediately send your exact indoor location to campus security and your emergency contacts. A staff member will come to your location. The app will tell you, "Help is on the way. Please stay where you are."

## 5. Schedule Integration

If your university schedule is linked to your account, the app knows where you need to be.
- When you open the app, you can double tap and say *"Next class"*. 
- The app will say, for example, "Your next class is Biology in Room 204. Starting navigation."

## 6. Obstacle Detection

The app uses the phone's sensors to detect if you might be walking into a wall or sudden drop.
- **To enable**: Double-tap and say, *"Enable obstacle detection."*
- When enabled, the phone will emit a rapid pulsing vibration if it detects an immediate hazard directly in front of you. 

## 7. Tips for the Best Experience

- **Keep Bluetooth On**: The app relies entirely on Bluetooth signals to know where you are.
- **Phone Placement**: The app works best when held in your hand or placed in a front chest pocket. Thick bags may block the Bluetooth signal.
- **Listen to your surroundings**: While the app provides guidance, always rely primarily on your white cane or guide dog for immediate physical safety.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does this use my cellular data?**
A: The app requires a very small amount of data to calculate routes and check your schedule. Once a route is started, it will continue to work even if you briefly lose internet connection.

**Q: What if the app says I am in the wrong place?**
A: Take a few steps forward. The Bluetooth signals can sometimes bounce. Moving slightly helps the app recalculate your exact position. If you are lost, you can always use the SOS feature.
